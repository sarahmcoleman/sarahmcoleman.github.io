Sarah M. Coleman, PhD

Bioprocess Development Engineer | Fermentation, process development, and the statistics behind both

I’m a bioprocess development engineer. My background spans upstream process development and fed-batch fermentation, synthetic biology and strain engineering, and the statistics and bioinformatics that support both, with experience across pharma (Bristol Myers Squibb, Takeda), academic (UT Austin, MIT), and startup (BioReNuva) environments.

At BioReNuva I developed the company’s first fed-batch bioreactor process and led its transfer to manufacturing. My doctoral work in the Alper lab centered on Yarrowia lipolytica: Golden Gate based genome editing and promoter library screening, adaptive laboratory evolution to raise tolerance to industrial waste streams, and CFD-informed scale-up of high-oil-density fermentations. I work across bacterial, yeast, and mammalian cell culture, and I picked up an MS in Statistics alongside the PhD.

I’m open to relocation nationwide and am available to start within 30 days.
